GP & Primary Care Survey

This short, anonymous survey is part of our work with Action Together to help reduce health inequalities in access to drug and alcohol recovery support for South Asian communities in Rochdale.

The questions are based on what The Salik Project UK is already hearing from local communities and focus groups — but we want to hear about your own experience as a GP or primary care professional. Your contribution will genuinely help us build a clearer picture and make the case for better, more culturally informed support locally.
